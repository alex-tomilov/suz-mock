# Code of Conduct

## Standards

Project spaces should stay professional, practical, and respectful. Good
participation includes:

- focusing feedback on code, documentation, and behavior;
- explaining tradeoffs clearly;
- accepting correction when facts change;
- keeping examples and reports free of private or production data.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, intimidation,
publishing private information, or repeatedly derailing technical discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate these standards. Maintainers may also block people
from project spaces when needed to keep the project workable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the hosting platform's private contact or
moderation tools when available. If private reporting is not available, contact
the maintainers through the least public project channel available and include
only the context needed to investigate.
